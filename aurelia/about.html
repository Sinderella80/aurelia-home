About us

A family workshop that believes furniture should get better with age.

Since 1998

Aurelia Home began as a two-person joinery in Damascus. Today our craftsmen build every sofa, table and bed by hand, using the same joints our grandfathers taught us.

Quiet luxury

We design in warm, natural tones because they last. No trends, no loud finishes. Just proportion, comfort and material you can feel.

Each piece carries the signature of the maker who built it.
